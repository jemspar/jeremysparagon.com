## React, HTML5, CSS3. *2020*

Rebuilt the website for the local construction company Keegan Construction, shifting the focus from a simple introduction to their company to a full portfolio that can be shown clients when planning new project.

Used React, Webpack, MDX, and YAML to create a fast, static site, while keeping the projects (and their hundreds of photos) organized and adaptable.
